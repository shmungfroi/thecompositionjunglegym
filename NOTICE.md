## Scores (Analysis room)

Each score in `scores/` is an SVG engraved from a Humdrum encoding, with timing, cadence and form annotations in `scores/meta.json`.

| Score(s) | Title and creator | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bach chorales | 370 Bach chorales, Humdrum encodings by Craig Stuart Sapp | https://github.com/craigsapp/bach-370-chorales | [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) |
| Mozart piano sonatas | Mozart piano sonatas, Humdrum encodings by Craig Stuart Sapp, from the Alte Mozart-Ausgabe (1878) | https://github.com/craigsapp/mozart-piano-sonatas | [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) |
| Joplin rags | Scott Joplin edition, Humdrum encodings by Craig Stuart Sapp | https://github.com/craigsapp/joplin | [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) |
| Chopin, Preludes Op. 28 Nos. 7 and 20 | The Fryderyk Chopin Institute (NIFC), Chopin first editions in Humdrum, Breitkopf & Härtel first edition (Leipzig, 1839) | https://github.com/pl-wnifc/humdrum-chopin-first-editions | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

**Changes made.** The Composition Jungle Gym converted the encodings and engraved them to SVG with Verovio, and added timing, cadence and form annotations.

**ShareAlike.** The score images (`scores/*.svg`) and the analysis data in `scores/meta.json` are derived from these encodings and are shared under the licence of their source: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for the Bach, Mozart and Joplin scores (including their annotations), and CC BY 4.0 for the NIFC Chopin scores. Verovio (LGPL) is only the engraving tool; it does not set the licence of the output.
