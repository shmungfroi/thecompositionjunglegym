Listening for it

  1. Keep the pulse. Tap the four beats with one hand while you listen.
  2. Say it back. Use time names (ta, ti-ti, ta-a) or count 1 & 2 & before you write anything.
  3. Write one beat at a time. Each tile is as wide as it is long, so a full bar fills the line.

The bar under the notes shows how long each sound lasts. A dashed gap is a rest. Dotted crotchet + quaver is long-short across two beats; quaver, crotchet, quaver is short-long-short.
