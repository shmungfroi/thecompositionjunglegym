Listening for it

  1. Find the heavy click. It marks beat 1. Tap it on your knee.
  2. Count to the next heavy click. Two, three or four beats give you duple, triple or quadruple.
  3. Listen inside one beat. Two even sounds is simple. Three is compound, with a rolling, skipping feel.

The speed changes every time (60–120 beats a minute), so fast or slow is never a clue. 6/8 is two beats of three quavers, not six beats.
