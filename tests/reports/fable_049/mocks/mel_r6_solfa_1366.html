Listening for it

  1. Sing it back in solfa before you answer. The key chord plays first and the first note is always given.
  2. Movable do. Do is home in a major key; in a minor key the home is la, and si is the raised 7th that leads up to la.
  3. Mind the octave marks. A comma marks the octave below (s, l, t,), an apostrophe the octave above (d').
