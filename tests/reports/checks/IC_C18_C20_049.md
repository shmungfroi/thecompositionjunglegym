## 2. Lint checks: FAIL (can false-pass)

The new checks were mutation-tested on full copies of the worktree:

| Mutation | Expected | Lint result |
|---|---|---|
| Base 049 `index.html` | fail ×3 | Fails on exactly Highway to Hell, Wonderwall and Misty. Correct. |
| Oasis entry in the Metal slot, `fallback` set | fail | Fails ("holds a genre fallback"). Correct. |
| Metal slot both filled and in SONGGAPS | fail | Fails. Correct. |
| SONGGAPS slot typo (`Metl`) | fail | Fails. Correct. |
| SONGGAPS const renamed | fail | Fails ("SONGGAPS data not found"). Correct. |
| **Oasis "Wonderwall" in the `aug` Metal slot, `fallback: null`, recording text "(Britpop rock, not metal)"** | fail | **PASSES (exit 0).** False pass. |

Causes:
- The regex is dead code. The condition is `x.get('fallback') or _FB.search(str(x.get('fallback') or ''))`. The regex runs only when `fallback` is empty, and then it searches `''`, which can never match. In practice the rule is just "the Metal slot has no fallback note". The regex never looks at `recording` or `suitability`, which is where the old data actually said "not metal", "Hard rock" or "Britpop".
- No lint can confirm that a song really is metal. The check enforces only that a stand-in is labelled. A non-metal song entered without a fallback note passes.
- The checks cover the Metal slot only. Blues/jazz holds labelled stand-ins (Learn to Fly in sus2 and Free Fallin' in sus4 are rock songs whose `fallback` notes say so) and the lint does not flag them. That is the same slot-honesty problem in another genre.
- Undeclared empty slots are not checked. Many chord types have core slots that are neither filled nor in SONGGAPS, so no gap card is shown for them. These slots and gaps predate this branch, but the "gaps shown honestly" rule is not enforced:
  `""` Blues/jazz and MT; `m` MT; `sus2` Metal; `aug` Blues/jazz, MT and Metal; `maj7` MT; `m7` Metal; `6` Blues/jazz, Rock, MT and Metal; `add9` MT; `madd9` Blues/jazz, Pop, Rock and MT; `m6` MT and Metal; `m7b5` Metal; `dim7` MT and Metal; `9` Blues/jazz, MT and Metal; `maj9` Pop, Rock, MT and Metal; `m9` Blues/jazz, Pop, MT and Metal; `7sus4` MT and Metal.

## 3. C20 Shape of You: PASS

These sources were fetched separately (none is a lyric site):
1. Hooktheory (https://www.hooktheory.com/theorytab/view/ed-sheeran/shape-of-you): C♯ minor, 96 BPM, 4/4. Intro and chorus are i–iv–VI–VII, verse and pre-chorus are i–iv–VI–VIIsus4. In C♯ minor that is C♯m–F♯m–A–B (Bsus4 in the verse).
2. 8notes play-along (https://www.8notes.com/scores/25594.asp): the chords C♯m, F♯m, A and B, repeated. It names no key and has no lyrics.
3. Music Will / Modern Band chart (https://jamzone.musicwill.org/Songchart/ModernBand-ShapeOfYou-EdSheeran.pdf): C♯ minor, 96 BPM, chord boxes for C♯m, F♯m, A and B, and no lyrics. I found this source myself; it is not in the builder report.
4. Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shape_of_You): C♯ minor, 96 BPM, C♯m–F♯m–A–B (i–iv–VI–VII). Its cited source is Musicnotes, so Wikipedia and Musicnotes count as one source between them, not two.

Three independent chord sources (1, 2 and 3) agree. The builder's reading of Am–Dm–F–G as the same progression transposed down a major third is correct (C♯→A, F♯→D, A→F, B→G). The song was correctly not added, because adding it would mean replacing another slot. That decision rests with Justin.
